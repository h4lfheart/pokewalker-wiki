# Specifications

## CPU

[`H8/300H`](/hardware/cpu/summary) 



This is in the "Super Low Power Series" of Renesas' 16-Bit Single-Chip Microcomputers within the [H8/38606](/hardware/microcomputer/summary) group.

## EEPROM

`ST M95512 64KB SPI`

The ST M95512 is a 512-Kbit (64-Kbyte) EEPROM using the SPI (Serial Peripheral Interface) bus.

## Accelerometer

`Bosch BMA150`

A 12-Pin LGA, three-axis accelerometer.
