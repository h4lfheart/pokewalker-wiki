## What is the Pokéwalker?

The **Pokéwalker** is a pedometer device for *Pokémon HeartGold* and *Pokémon SoulSilver* released by Nintendo in 2009. It interacts with the games via infrared, tracks real-world steps, transfers Pokémon, and lets you play minigames to earn Pokémon and items.
